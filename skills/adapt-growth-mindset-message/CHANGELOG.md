# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The lesson opens with a few plain sentences to the user (whether the school lets effort pay off, what is still unknown, and that no effect is promised), and four labels are plain: "What the school offers (gate)" becomes "Does the school give students a way to improve?", "Check on delivery only" becomes "Shows only that the lesson was received", "The record" becomes "What trials have found", and "Pre-test before use" becomes "A small test before use". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The lesson is now written only when students' own words name the question and the school gives them a way to improve; the output states the record and no longer gives a planning effect size. The evidence was rechecked against each source: figures that could not be read in a source were removed, and a trial in rural China was added. Rewritten with Claude.
