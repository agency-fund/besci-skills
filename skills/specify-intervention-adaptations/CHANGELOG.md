# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The record opens with a few plain sentences (whether the changed version is still the one tested, the change that matters most, and what would settle the unknowns) and uses plainer labels: "as tested" for "baseline", which an M&E reader could take for a baseline survey; "core components: the parts the effect depends on"; "applies to", "kind of change", and "fits the core components?" for FRAME's "level", "nature", and "fidelity" columns; and "changes that put a job at risk" for "flags". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard. The record now writes each core component's job, the assumption behind it, and its permissible and prohibited variation (MRC framework) before logging any change, logs changes on the eight FRAME fields with "unknown" as a fidelity mark, and follows the four ADAPT steps for a move to a new context, all checked against the full texts. Rewritten with Claude.
