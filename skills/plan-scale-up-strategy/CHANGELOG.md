# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The strategy opens with a few plain sentences (who will own the program, the test or change it hangs on, and the date that cannot slip), and the guide's terms are glossed where they appear: vertical and horizontal scaling, hardware and software, "The innovation: the program and its parts", "The user organization: who will run it routinely", "The resource team: who supports the move, and until when", "Spontaneous scaling: copies nobody planned", and "Operational plan: who does what, by when"; "Judgment tested?" becomes "Was that tested?". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard. The strategy now follows the nine steps of the WHO and ExpandNet guide by name, including its table of system changes needed to institutionalize a program, and ends in prioritized recommendations and an operational plan in which every action has an owner and a date. The skill now declares its output as `scale-up-strategy`; the unverified Management Systems International framework is no longer cited. Rewritten with Claude.
