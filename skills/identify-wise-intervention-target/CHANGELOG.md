# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The analysis opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the question the audience is asking, any reading that is a material barrier instead, and whether the setting can back a new answer) and takes a plain title for "Wise-intervention target". Labels are plain: each of the three needs is glossed with its question, "When it becomes live" becomes "When people start asking it", the two "Verdict" labels become "Material, meaning, or both" and "Does the setting deliver?", and "The record for this kind of intervention" becomes "What trials of this kind of intervention found". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-10
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, about 40% shorter. The question is now named under one of Walton and Wilson's three needs (to understand, self-integrity, to belong) in place of the earlier four labels. The evidence was rechecked against each source's abstract, and a World Bank brief on Indonesia was added. Rewritten with Claude.
