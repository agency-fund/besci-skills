# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The theory of change opens with a few plain sentences to the user (how many links rest on data, the weakest link nearest the outcome, and what would strengthen it), and its title names the program; the labels were already plain, and the four strength labels are unchanged. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The sources are now Mayne (2015), the Medical Research Council framework, and the Busara toolkit, each read and checked; the Kellogg guide, the analysis plan template, and the paragraphs on how published effects shrink were removed. The four strength labels are now defined by where the data came from, and the template asks for anything outside the program that could move the outcome. Rewritten with Claude.
