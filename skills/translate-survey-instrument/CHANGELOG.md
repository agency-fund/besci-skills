# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The record opens with a few plain sentences to the user (where the translation stands, the items most likely to shift, and that it is untested); "Translations and reconciliation" becomes "The two translations and the version agreed", "reconciled" in the status becomes "agreed by the reviewers", and "Register" and "Back-translation" are glossed where they first appear. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The output is now a translation record that can be filled before or after the translators work, the AI's own target-language wording is labeled a draft, and the worked example shows translations as English glosses. The evidence was rechecked: the claim that back-translation misses problems now rests on two experiments and a re-analysis that were read, the team method is cited from the European Social Survey's guidelines, and sources whose text could not be read were removed. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
