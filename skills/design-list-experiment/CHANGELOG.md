# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The design opens with a few plain sentences to the user (whether a list experiment is worth it at this sample, the likeliest way it fails here, and that it gives a share for the group, never for a person); "Respondents and mode" becomes "Respondents and how they are surveyed", control statements are glossed as the harmless ones, and the design-effect test is glossed with the question it answers. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The go or no-go step now uses thresholds read in the 2020 meta-analysis (about 14 times the variance of a direct question; the under-reporting needed at 1,000 and 2,000 respondents), where the earlier draft said only "a multiple". Field studies from Nicaragua, Senegal, Nigeria, Peru, South Africa, Kenya, and Zambia were added, and the unsourced rules on item topic and item position were dropped. Rewritten with Claude.
