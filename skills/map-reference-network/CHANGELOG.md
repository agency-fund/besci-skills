# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The map opens with a few plain sentences to the user (whom people would follow, who can block, and how that compares with whom the program reaches), takes a plain title for "Reference network map", and renames its shorthand labels: "How the names were collected" for "Design", with each approach glossed; "Who they named, by role" for "Reference group by role"; "Does it themselves" and "Approves of it" for "Does X" and "Approves of X"; "Who sways the decision, and who can block it" for "Three lists"; and "Who the program reaches, and who it misses" for "Reference group against the program's reach". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, about 40% shorter. Interview evidence that other people matter is now enough to start, and snowball replaces egocentric as the usual design, as the UNICEF manual advises. That manual was opened and is now cited for the three designs, and a review of network studies in low- and middle-income countries and studies from India and Honduras were added. Rewritten with Claude.
