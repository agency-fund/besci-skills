# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The design choice opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the design, what decided it, and what is still unknown) and is titled "How to randomize the test". Shorthand labels became plain: "Withholding" is "Can some wait or go without?", "Control" is "What the program controls", "Arms and allocation" is "Groups compared, and how many units in each", "Delay or withholding agreed by" is "Who agreed that some wait or go without", and spillovers, the unit of randomization, stratification, and each design are glossed where they appear. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11

It adds a multi-arm row and says plainly when no randomized design fits, so a quasi-experimental design and a specialist are needed; the hand-offs to other skills, the power arithmetic, and the analysis notes are gone. Sources were re-verified and cut to the four the method rests on. Rewritten with Claude.
