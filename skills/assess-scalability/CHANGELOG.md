# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The assessment opens with a few plain sentences (the verdict, the difference that decided it, and that it judges whether the effect holds, not its size), is titled as the question it answers, and uses plainer labels: "the pilot compared with delivery at scale" for "pilot against scale", "what we compared" for "row", and "the test I'd run first" for "test first". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard. The assessment now compares the pilot as run with delivery at scale on seven fixed rows drawn from the ExpandNet guides and BIT's SCALE factors, counts any untested difference as a minus, and gives one of the three verdicts of the Intervention Scalability Assessment Tool with no total score; the generic domain-score table and the discounting of the pilot effect are gone. Rewritten with Claude.
