# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The plan opens with a few plain sentences (which parts it watches most closely, the check that does not rest on the deliverers' own records, and what the data may be used for), is titled "How we'll know <the program> is delivered as designed", and uses plainer labels: "what it changes for the person receiving it" for "mechanism it carries", "what it checks" for "dimension", with adherence, dose, and responsiveness glossed, and "what an evaluation also needs to track" for "what an evaluation needs caught". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.
