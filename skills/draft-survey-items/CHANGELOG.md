# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The draft opens with a few plain sentences to the user (how many questions for which constructs, anything left unwritten, and that they are untested), and "Questions by construct" becomes "Questions, by what each one measures". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length, with a fixed eight-rule design check and a "Not written here" section in the output. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the Kenya study recommends keeping reversed items in agree/disagree scales so that yea-saying can be measured, so the blanket ban on them now has that exception; five answer options are the default, following a randomized comparison in six African countries, with a three-option version for respondents who cannot read, following two studies of non-readers; and the experiments on agree/disagree statements are reported as mixed. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
First draft. Drafted with Claude from the verified evidence dossier (`docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md`) for Zezhen Wu's review; four evals; not yet reviewed.
