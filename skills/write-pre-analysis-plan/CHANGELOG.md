# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The plan opens with a few plain sentences to the user: what it commits to, where it adds detail because someone has a stake, and that it must be registered before anyone sees outcome data by arm. "Estimator" is now "How the effect is estimated (the estimator)", and "arm", "outcome families", and "standard errors" are glossed where they first appear; the title keeps "pre-analysis plan", the term registries use. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, with one job: fixing the analysis before the data arrive. The plan now names the multiple-testing method for each outcome family itself instead of handing it to another skill, checks which registry applies (including the clinical-registry rule for health journals), and draws on Brodeur et al. (2024), who found that registration alone showed no sign of reducing p-hacking while a full plan did. Citations were re-verified: Banerjee et al. (2020) now lists its six authors, and the OES template and EGAP guide were dropped. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
First draft. Drafted with Claude from the verified evidence dossier (`docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md`) for Zezhen Wu's review; four evals; not yet reviewed.
