# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The output opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the proposed default and who gets something else, what makes it fair, and whether a specialist must review it), is titled "What people get if they do nothing" instead of "Default option specification", and uses plain labels: "Why a default would work here" for "Mechanism", with endorsement, endowment, and ease each glossed; "Are people who stayed in worse off?" for "Harm check"; and "Whose interest it serves" for "Serves the chooser because". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-10
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the meta-analysis has four authors, not five, and names its third channel "ease"; field trials from India and Malawi join the Afghanistan one. The skill no longer quotes a discounted effect size; it states the direction the evidence supports. Rewritten with Claude.
