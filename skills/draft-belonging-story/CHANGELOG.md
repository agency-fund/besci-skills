# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The set opens with a few plain sentences to the user (whether the setting includes the group, where the stories come from, and that no effect is promised), its title "Belonging story set" becomes "Stories from older peers for <the newcomers>", and six labels are plain: "What the setting offers (gate)" becomes "Does the setting include them?", "Collection" becomes "Collecting the stories", "Framing line" becomes "The line that introduces the stories", "Check on delivery only" becomes "Shows only that the stories landed", "The record" becomes "What trials have found", and "Pre-test before use" becomes "A small test before use". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. Stories now come only from accounts the team has collected, and a story the skill writes to show the shape is labeled an illustration; no stories are written where the group is harassed or excluded. The evidence was rechecked against each source, the sources that describe how the exercise is built were added, and the output no longer gives a planning effect size. Rewritten with Claude.
