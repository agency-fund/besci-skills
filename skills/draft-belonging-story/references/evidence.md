# Evidence: Peer stories that make early struggle feel normal

## Sources

- Walton, G. M., & Cohen, G. L. (2011). A brief social-belonging intervention improves academic and health outcomes of minority students. *Science*, 331(6023), 1447–1451. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1198364 Supports: the message. The exercise aimed to lessen perceived threat "by framing social adversity as common and transient", and "used subtle attitude-change strategies to lead participants to self-generate the intervention message". Abstract only.
- Murphy, M. C., Gopalan, M., Carter, E. R., Emerson, K. T. U., Bottoms, B. L., & Walton, G. M. (2020). A customized belonging intervention improves retention of socially disadvantaged students at a broad-access university. *Science Advances*, 6(29). https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aba4677 Supports: collecting stories locally, and the order of the exercise. The team rebuilt materials from selective universities with older students and administrators at the university, using surveys and focus groups, so that the stories named the difficulties of that place, such as long commutes and several jobs. In a one-hour class, first-year students read nine stories from older students, wrote about how their own experience matched, then wrote a letter to a future student who doubted they belonged. Full text.
- Yeager, D. S., Walton, G. M., Brady, S. T., Akcinar, E. N., Paunesku, D., Keane, L., et al. (2016). Teaching a lay theory before college narrows achievement gaps at scale. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, 113(24). https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1524360113 Supports: the advice task. The paper calls the writing step "saying-is-believing" and says it "allows students to personalize generic materials, promoting internalization". Students were told their essays might be shared with future students, and some were. Full text.
- Walton, G. M., Murphy, M. C., Logel, C., Yeager, D. S., Goyer, J. P., Brady, S. T., et al. (2023). Where and with whom does a brief social-belonging intervention promote progress in college? *Science*, 380(6644), 499–505. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ade4420 Supports: the check of the setting. Abstract only. An erratum (https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ads9718) corrects two transposed values in a supplementary table and states that no conclusion changes.
- Walton, G. M., & Wilson, T. D. (2018). Wise interventions: Psychological remedies for social and personal problems. *Psychological Review*, 125(5), 617–655. https://doi.org/10.1037/rev0000115 Supports: the framework, in which an exercise changes the meaning people draw about themselves or their situation, and an early change can become embedded. Abstract only.

The four parts a story must have, the rule that nothing is invented, and the pre-test are this skill's own.

The scan found no meta-analysis of this exercise and no field trial in a low- or middle-income country. Searches covered India, Africa, Latin America, and China, and replications in the Netherlands, Australia, the United Kingdom, and Canada. What depends on the place is whether the setting includes the group in fact, and which line divides newcomers there: region, language, caste, gender, or being first in the family.

## Replication

Replicated in the United States, with a condition. The first study was small and its effect large. Later trials with thousands of students found gains in enrollment and completion for disadvantaged groups, and the largest found them only where those groups had real opportunities to belong. One large single-university trial reports no gain from its belonging exercise. Outside the United States the record is one small trial with mixed results.

## What to check locally

1. Which line divides newcomers here, in their own words; the tellers must come from the same side of it.
2. Whether named people or arrangements include the group, and whether anyone has asked the group about harassment.
3. Whether older peers of the group exist; if this intake is the first of its kind, the exercise cannot run as tested.
4. When the first setback comes in the calendar, so that delivery comes before it.
5. Whether written stories suit the reading level, or a session led by older peers is needed.
