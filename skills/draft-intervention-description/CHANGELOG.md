# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The description opens with a few plain sentences (whether a team elsewhere could deliver it again, the gap that matters most, and which items rest on memory), and three of TIDieR's terms are glossed where they appear: "Tailoring: variation planned before delivery", "Modifications: changes made during delivery", and "Rebuild check: could a team elsewhere deliver it again?". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.
