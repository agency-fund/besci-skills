# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The decision opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the wording to use, what decided it, what was taken out of the loss version, and that no difference is promised), its title "Message frame decision" becomes "Gain or loss wording for <the message>", and "Vehicle" becomes "Words or money", in the template and in step 2. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at a little over half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the finding that most workers prefer gain-framed pay belongs to Ferraro and Tracy's own experiment, not to the Uganda trial; three more reviews and an 84-country experiment were added; and field trials from India and China join the ones already cited. The skill no longer quotes an expected difference between frames; it makes the gain version the default and promises no difference. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
First draft. Drafted with Claude from the verified evidence dossier (`docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md`) for Zezhen Wu's review; four evals; not yet reviewed.
