# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The phone version opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the call length and what was cut, who the calls will most likely miss, and that a change since the face-to-face round may come from the phone), "Mode" becomes "Call type", and the mode check is glossed as "does the phone change the answers?". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the World Bank handbook is now cited with its authors in the order on its title page (Dabalen first) and supplies the 15 to 30 minute rule of thumb the earlier draft could not source; a 2026 review of phone surveys in 21 low- and middle-income countries and field comparisons from Nigeria, Burkina Faso, Ethiopia, and South Africa were added; and the two-step scale is now tied to Krosnick and Presser. Rewritten with Claude.
