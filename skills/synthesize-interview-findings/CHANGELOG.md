# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard and about 40% shorter. Each verdict now weighs the three tests from the TDF guide (how often a belief comes up, whether accounts conflict, how strongly it is held) and compares people who do the behavior with people who do not; a hypothesis the interviews did not ask about is "not tested". The speed-versus-depth section and the rapid-methods, thematic-analysis, and implementation-research sources are gone, the UNICEF guidance is added, and the rule against reporting coder agreement is dropped because the TDF guide recommends it. Rewritten with Claude.
