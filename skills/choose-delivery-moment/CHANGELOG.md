# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The output opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the moment, what decided it, who it leaves out, and that no effect is promised), its title "Delivery moment specification" becomes "When to <the ask>", and the calendar's "Position" column becomes "Flush or short?". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length, with one produced output where the first draft declared none. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: effect sizes for the Kenyan fertilizer trial were dropped because they could not be confirmed in the published article, field trials from Kenya and Colombia were added, and the South African test of fresh-start messages is reported as inconclusive. The skill tests each candidate moment on money, attention, and whether the action can be done, and no longer points to skills that have been removed. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
