## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at a little over half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the 2019 gossips paper, which the first draft said had no usable results, reports 22% more children vaccinated in Haryana villages where named villagers received the information; the Malawi communicator trial and a United States trial of lay and expert senders were added; and the cost-effectiveness figure for a bundle of interventions is no longer presented as evidence for the messenger. The skill now says plainly that no review compares kinds of messenger, and that the rule matching messenger to barrier is its own. Rewritten with Claude.
