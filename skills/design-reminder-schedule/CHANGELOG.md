# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The schedule opens with a few plain sentences to the user (how many reminders, when, by what channel, who they miss, and whether reminders are enough alone), and the table heading "Sends" becomes "The reminders", here and where the specialist section refers to it; the other labels were already plain. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at well under half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the Kenyan trial's 53% figure, which the first draft told the model to refuse, is in the paper's abstract (53% against 40%), and the finding that weekly texts beat daily ones is in the Cochrane review; reviews and null trials from low- and middle-income countries were added. The skill no longer quotes a planning range for the effect; it states the direction the evidence supports. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
