# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The output opens with a few plain sentences to the user (what the copy asks of people, what it must contain, and that it waits on the test in the final language), is titled "Draft wording" instead of "Intervention content draft", and uses plain labels: "What each part of the draft delivers" for "Trace", "How often is too often" for "Frequency ceiling", and the EAST check glossed as "is it easy, attractive, social, and timely?". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about 60% of its earlier length. The draft is now tied to "what the technique must contain", copied from a published definition when one is open and marked as a paraphrase when not, so the technique need not come from one taxonomy. Drafting rules and the test before launch now follow the Behavioural Insights Team's guides and Intervention Mapping; the evidence was rechecked, and a pretesting study is now cited for what its abstract reports. Zezhen Wu added as co-author. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-08
Ported from Joe Speed's prototype (joe-speed/behavioural-skills, `lever-content-drafter` v0.1.1, commit 68257d1) by Zezhen Wu on Joe's behalf. Restructured to this library's eight sections: added Before you start, How to do it, When to bring in a specialist, a replication note, and four evals; the worked example moved to `references/worked-example.md`; American spelling. Method content is the original author's. Not yet reviewed.
