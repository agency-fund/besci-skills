A search finds what was published and indexed. For most program settings outside wealthy Western countries, that is a small share of what has been tried.

## Replication

Not applicable. A rapid review is a method. Its known weakness is coverage: two time-bounded scans of one question can return different studies.

## What to check locally

1. Whether evaluations exist that were never published in journals: government reports, donor evaluations, and the implementer's own monitoring.
2. Whether relevant studies exist in languages the search did not use.
3. Whether a researcher who works in the country knows of trials in progress.
