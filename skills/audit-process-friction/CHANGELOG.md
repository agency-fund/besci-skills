# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The audit opens with a few plain sentences to the user (what finishing costs one person, where people are lost, which change heads the list), takes a plain title ("What it takes to <finish the process>, step by step" for "Process friction audit"), and names its step-log columns by what they hold ("Keep, remove, move, or check" for "Tag", "Why" for "Reason for the tag", "People in → out" for "In → out"). The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, about 40% shorter. The steps are now tagged keep, remove, move, or check, and the audit records who is lost at each step. The evidence was rechecked against each source: Sunstein's article is dated 2022 with its volume and pages, the Kenya figures and the discount on published effects are gone, and studies from Morocco, Indonesia, and South Africa were added. Rewritten with Claude.
