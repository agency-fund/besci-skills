# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24
First version, and the first supporting skill in the stage `other` (ADR 0011). Carries Zezhen Wu's revision rules for research prose as `references/academic-voice.md`, which is kept identical to `template/shared/academic-voice.md` so that report-writing skills can bundle the same rules. Adds the inventory of citations, numbers, quotations, and hedges that is taken before writing and checked after. The three published sources were confirmed against Crossref or the proceedings page; the word list in Kobak et al. (2025) was read from the authors' published data. Adapted and drafted with Claude.
