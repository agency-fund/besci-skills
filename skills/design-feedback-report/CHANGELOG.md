# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources and widened: the 2025 Cochrane review of feedback to health professionals and field trials from Zambia, Mali, India, Costa Rica, and Colombia were added, and the India teacher study's finding (no gain in learning) is now stated. The comparison rule changed: a benchmark or top peers come before the peer average, and the skill no longer treats a sign of approval as a reliable guard against better performers slipping, because a much larger study did not find that it worked. Rewritten with Claude.
