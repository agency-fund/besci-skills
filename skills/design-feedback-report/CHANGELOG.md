# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The design opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the number, its comparison, how the top and bottom are treated, who else sees it, and that no effect is promised). Its title "Feedback report specification" becomes "A feedback report for <who>"; under the comparison, "Type" becomes "Compared with" and "Reference" becomes "Which standard or group"; "Report content" becomes "What the report says", "Frequency and end" becomes "How often, and when it stops", and "Read separately" becomes "Results read separately for". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources and widened: the 2025 Cochrane review of feedback to health professionals and field trials from Zambia, Mali, India, Costa Rica, and Colombia were added, and the India teacher study's finding (no gain in learning) is now stated. The comparison rule changed: a benchmark or top peers come before the peer average, and the skill no longer treats a sign of approval as a reliable guard against better performers slipping, because a much larger study did not find that it worked. Rewritten with Claude.
