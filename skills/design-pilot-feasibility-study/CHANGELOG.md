# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The pilot plan opens with a few plain sentences to the user: the rate the pilot most needs to pin down, the line that would change the main study, and that the pilot will not show whether the program works. "What it serves" is now "What it prepares for", "Main-trial inputs" is "Figures the main trial needs", progression criteria are glossed as go, amend, and stop lines, and "Report skeleton" is "Outline of the pilot report". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard. The pilot is now sized by the 95% margin on its most important rate, picks typical sites and routine conditions as WHO and ExpandNet advise, and lists the main trial's inputs it will measure; go, amend, and stop now cite the CONSORT pilot extension, which names all three, and the five headings cite the MRC framework. The neighbor for trying a draft with a few people is now `plan-prototype-test`, and the output is a new `pilot-plan`. Rewritten with Claude.
