# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24
First version. Runs the six phases of Braun and Clarke (2006) with AI agents, either with the researcher deciding at three checkpoints or on its own, and tells the user at the start that the method's authors reject generative AI for reflexive thematic analysis (Jowsey et al., 2026). Three scripts ship with it: `check_extracts.py` (every extract and report quote against the data), `draw_map.py` (the thematic map as PNG, SVG, and Mermaid), and `build_report.py` (the report as an APA-style Word file). Written from the two method papers; sources were confirmed against Crossref records and the pages themselves, and each entry in `references/evidence.md` says how much of the source was read. Drafted with Claude.
