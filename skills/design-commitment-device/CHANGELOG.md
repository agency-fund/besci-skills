# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The design opens with a few plain sentences to the user (the device, the most anyone can lose, and that many will decline and no effect is promised), its title "Commitment device specification" becomes "A commitment device for <the behavior>", "Type" becomes "Soft or hard", with both kinds explained in the template, and "Cap" becomes "The most anyone can lose". The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources and widened: the smoking trial's effect is the 3 percentage points its abstract reports, and trials from the Philippines, India, and Uganda in which committers lost money or a stricter device did worse now shape the cap, the plan for people who fail, and the review rule. The skill no longer gives an expected take-up or effect; it states the direction the evidence supports. Rewritten with Claude.
