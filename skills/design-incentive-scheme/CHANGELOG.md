# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-11
Follows the conversational-voice standard (ADR 0009). The scheme opens with a few plain sentences to the user (what is paid for which checked action, how it compares with the cost of acting and a day's income, what happens at the end, and that no effect is promised), and its title "Incentive scheme specification" becomes "An incentive for <the behavior>"; the other labels were already plain. The output ends with next moves and their reasons, chosen from candidates listed in the closing, and the skill carries the shared voice block. The method is unchanged. Edited with Claude.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-11
Rewritten to the November 2026 standard, at about half its earlier length. The evidence was rechecked against the sources: the claim that unconditional transfers raise enrollment odds by 18 to 25% was dropped because the review's abstract does not give it, the Rajasthan cost figures now read $28 and $56 per immunization as the abstract has them, and a 2025 review of immunization incentives in 19 low- and middle-income countries was added. The skill now covers penalties, names trials where a fine or an added payment backfired, and no longer quotes a discounted effect size. Rewritten with Claude.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12
First draft. Drafted with Claude from the verified evidence dossier (`docs/plan/2026-10-skill-expansion-dossier.md`) for Zezhen Wu's review; four evals; not yet reviewed.
