# Canonical sources

An atomic skill earns its place when its task is a named step or tool in two or more of the sources on this page (`docs/skill-spec.md`, section 1). The list is fixed so that the test gives the same answer whoever applies it. Change it by pull request, with agreement from maintainers of both organizations.

Each citation below was checked against the publisher's page or the DOI record in November 2026. "Read" says how much of the source the check behind the first triage could reach; `docs/plan/2026-11-skill-triage.md` holds the task table drawn from them.

## Frameworks the library carries by choice

A skill built on one of these skips the "common" test only. It still has to state what it adds and be distinct, and it says whose method it is.

| Framework | Why the library carries it | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wise interventions | A distinct way of thinking about behavior change, through the meaning people make of themselves and their situation, that the process frameworks above do not cover. | Walton & Wilson (2018), above |
| The Agency Fund's agency framework | The definition and measurement of agency are central to the work of one of the library's two founding organizations. | The Agency Fund. Cited in the two agency skills. |
| The Agency Fund's AI evaluation playbook | Teams that build AI tools for social programs need a way to decide what to evaluate next. One skill carries the playbook's main idea. | Wu, Z., On, R., Walsh, J., Korley, E., Madon, T., & Wong, L. (2025). *AI Evaluation in the Social Sector: A Living Playbook*. https://eval.playbook.org.ai/ |
| Busara's SIFA | The library asks users to disclose how people and AI contributed to a piece of work; SIFA is the format it points to. | Busara. Cited in `draft-sifa-declaration`. |

## Two limits of this list

- **It is thin on analysis.** Only JPAL and MRC say anything about analyzing results. A statistical procedure can fail the two-source test for that reason alone. A later batch of analysis and methods skills should add sources first.
- **Several sources were only partly readable.** A task named in an unread chapter was not counted. A skill parked for lack of a second source can be reopened with the page that names it.
